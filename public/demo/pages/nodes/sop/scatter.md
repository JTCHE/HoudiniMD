Scatter drops new points on a surface or in a volume: the targets of a [Copy to Points](/nodes/sop/copytopoints), the seeds of a fracture.

![Points scattered over a shape, denser at the top](demo/scatter.svg)

## Density

**Density Attribute** names a float attribute on the input. Where it is 0 there are no points, and where it is 1 there are the most.
